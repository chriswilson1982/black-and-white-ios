Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Black & White doesn't collect your personal data and doesn't track you, and the developer never receives any information about you. The game may show a small banner promoting another independent app, through Kickstart Exchange, a free and privacy-conscious cross-promotion service for indie developers that matches apps, not people.

What the app stores

On your device

Your settings, your recent and best scores, and which achievements you've earned are saved on your device so the game can show them. They stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app.

iCloud

Your personal best for each difficulty, and your progress towards two achievements (how many stars you've collected and games you've played), are saved in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they're available on your other devices. Only you and your devices can access them. You can turn iCloud for the app off in the Settings app.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards and in your profile. This is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Banners for other apps

Black & White uses Kickstart Exchange to show banners for other independent apps, and in return other apps show banners for this one. To choose and count banners, the game sends Kickstart Exchange details about the app, not about you: the app's identity and version, your platform, and your App Store country. It also sends a one-off check from Apple that this is a genuine copy of the app, which Kickstart Exchange doesn't keep.

Kickstart Exchange records which banners are shown and tapped for about 30 days, without anything that identifies you or your device, and doesn't use this information to track you. If you tap a banner, it opens that app's page on the App Store. For full details, see the Kickstart Exchange privacy notice.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome via GitHub.
